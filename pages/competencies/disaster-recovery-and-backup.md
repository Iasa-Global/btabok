Disaster Recovery (DR) and Backup are essential components of an organization's business continuity strategy. DR focuses on restoring IT services after a disruptive event such as hardware failure, cyberattack, or natural disaster. Backup ensures that data is preserved and can be restored in the event of corruption, deletion, or system loss. While often considered interchangeable with Business Continuity Planning (BCP), DR is a subset focused specifically on IT recovery. Architects in this domain must design resilient systems, define recovery objectives, and integrate recovery planning with enterprise risk management and operational architecture.
