Software Architecture Tools enable architects to carry out core activities such as design, analysis, documentation, and evaluation. These tools support visualization (e.g. models, diagrams), decision tracking, trade-off analysis, quality attribute assessment, and alignment with frameworks or methodologies. They range from general-purpose modeling tools to specialized platforms for architecture governance, scenario simulation, or pattern selection. As systems grow in complexity and scope, tool proficiency becomes essential for maintaining clarity, traceability, and alignment with enterprise goals.
