System Management and Services refers to the set of operational capabilities required to monitor, maintain, and manage IT systems effectively. It includes tasks such as configuration management, performance monitoring, alerting, logging, patching, and service orchestration. The goal is to ensure that systems operate reliably, securely, and efficiently throughout their lifecycle. Architects in this domain focus on aligning operational architecture with system design, integrating tooling, and supporting service level objectives. This competency is increasingly integrated with DevOps, IT automation, and cloud-native operations.
