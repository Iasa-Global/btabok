Financial Methods encompass the tools and techniques architects use to understand and influence the financial impact of architectural decisions. These include standard financial analyses, cost modeling, and structural performance measures. Architects must grasp how initiatives affect capital and operational spending, investment return, and financial health. Mastery enables architects to support value-driven decision-making and guide the organization in aligning investments with strategic priorities.
