Advanced Quality Attributes (QAs) go beyond basic awareness of system performance or reliability to include deep analysis, prioritization, and trade-off decisions across multiple extra-functional requirements. Architects in this domain identify Architecturally Significant Requirements (ASRs), facilitate Quality Attribute Workshops (QAWs), and support decision-making through explicit analysis of QA impacts. This competency reflects the architect’s ability to model cost-benefit trade-offs, recognize interdependencies between QAs, and prioritize architectural choices based on business and technical context.
