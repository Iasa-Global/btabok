This competency centers on the architect’s ability to shape long-term technology direction and promote innovation aligned with organizational goals. It involves crafting and communicating strategic technology plans and fostering a culture that identifies, evaluates, and implements emerging technologies. Architects bridge business needs with IT capabilities, ensuring strategies are both visionary and actionable. Innovation requires not only creativity but also structured thinking and alignment with enterprise architecture.
