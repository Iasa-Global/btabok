This competency encompasses the principles, methods, and practices used to design, build, and evolve software systems with engineering rigor. It bridges software engineering disciplines—such as system design, maintenance, and evolution—with architectural thinking that emphasizes strategic value, stakeholder alignment, and technology enablement. Architects in this area apply engineering principles to ensure software systems are reliable, maintainable, and aligned with business goals throughout their lifecycle. They focus on delivering long-term value through structured planning, validated design, and scalable implementation.
