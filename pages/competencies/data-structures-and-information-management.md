This competency addresses the architectural practices that enable the creation, capture, transformation, and dissemination of data, information, and knowledge across an organization. It integrates data management principles with knowledge sharing processes, particularly within software development and engineering contexts. Architects in this area create systems and environments that support the dynamic cycle of tacit and explicit knowledge—ensuring that both human and machine-based knowledge assets are discoverable, reusable, and aligned with enterprise performance goals. The focus is on enabling insight through both structured data and experiential knowledge.
