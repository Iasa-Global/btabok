Information Governance is the framework of processes, policies, and responsibilities used to manage information throughout its lifecycle to ensure its accuracy, availability, security, and compliance. It aligns information practices with business objectives, legal requirements, and ethical standards. Architects in this area define structures that support responsible data ownership, stewardship, and access control, integrating governance into system design and data operations. Effective information governance enables trust, compliance, and the strategic use of information across the enterprise.
