GRC enables architects to align architectural work with organizational accountability, regulatory requirements, and enterprise objectives. It involves defining processes and structures to monitor performance, manage risk, and ensure compliance with laws, regulations, contracts, and policies. Architects use frameworks like RACI to clarify ownership and responsibility, improve decision-making, and support enterprise governance. This competency ensures architecture is trustworthy, traceable, and aligned with business integrity and accountability standards.
