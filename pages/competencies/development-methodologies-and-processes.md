This competency focuses on the architect's understanding and application of software development practices, lifecycle models, and team workflows. It includes knowledge of agile, DevOps, waterfall, and hybrid models, as well as familiarity with CI/CD pipelines, backlog management, and iterative delivery. Architects in this area bridge technical design and execution, ensuring that architectural decisions align with development velocity, quality goals, and operational realities. Mastery of this competency enables architects to influence team productivity, code quality, and the effective translation of design into working software.
