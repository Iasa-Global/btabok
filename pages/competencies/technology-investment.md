Technology Investment involves the justification, alignment, and management of IT spending as a driver of business performance. Architects help ensure that IT investments are strategically aligned with business goals and are managed as part of a broader portfolio of change initiatives. This competency includes understanding investment motivations, assessing value contribution, and using planning tools like Balanced Scorecards and Strategy Maps to support decision-making and prioritization.
