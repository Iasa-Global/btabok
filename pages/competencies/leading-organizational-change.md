Architects play a key role in navigating and enabling organizational change driven by globalization, digital disruption, workforce shifts, and strategic transformation. This competency focuses on the architect’s ability to lead through change—guiding stakeholders, aligning initiatives, and balancing innovation with continuity. It includes awareness of change impacts, communication strategies, and the architect’s role in supporting adoption and cultural transformation.
