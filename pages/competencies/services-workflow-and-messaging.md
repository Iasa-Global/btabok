This competency focuses on the architect’s ability to design and manage the components that structure service-based interactions, orchestrate business workflows, and ensure reliable communication through messaging systems. These elements are foundational to modern distributed systems, where processes span multiple services, users, and systems. Architects working in this area define service boundaries, coordinate service interactions, and implement asynchronous or event-driven patterns to support scalability, modularity, and resilience. This includes understanding how workflows are executed and how messaging frameworks connect services across platforms and domains.
