Information Management is the practice of acquiring, organizing, maintaining, and governing information to ensure its availability, usability, and integrity across the organization. It involves controlling how information is created, stored, distributed, and retired to support business decisions and operations. Architects in this domain enable strategic value by aligning information practices with enterprise goals, promoting data stewardship, and ensuring compliance with governance and lifecycle policies. Effective information management requires a holistic architectural approach that balances performance, usability, cost, and long-term value.
