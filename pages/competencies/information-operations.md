Information Operations focuses on the design, oversight, and control of how information flows through business operations. It involves ensuring that information services are efficient, reliable, and aligned with organizational goals. This competency addresses the operational qualities of information systems—such as effectiveness, sustainability, and robustness—and supports the ability of the enterprise to deliver consistent, accurate, and actionable data. Architects working in this area define and manage the distribution, availability, and quality of information across systems, helping maintain the health and agility of the broader architecture.
