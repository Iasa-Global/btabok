Information Value addresses the strategic importance and utility of information within an organization. It considers how information transitions from raw data to structured knowledge, supporting decisions, innovation, and operational outcomes. This competency involves evaluating the costs, exclusivity, context, and timeliness of information to determine its relevance and impact. Architects in this area assess how information supports business goals, drives outcomes, and delivers a return on investment. Understanding information value is essential to prioritize data initiatives, inform design decisions, and align architecture with measurable business impact.
