This competency focuses on the architect’s understanding and application of reusable design patterns and architectural styles to solve recurring problems in software architecture. It includes the use of classical object-oriented patterns, system-level patterns (e.g., microservices, layered architecture), and broader architectural styles (e.g., event-driven, service-oriented). Architects apply these tools to enhance consistency, reduce complexity, and improve quality attributes such as scalability, maintainability, and adaptability. Effective use of patterns demonstrates architectural thinking that leverages industry knowledge to accelerate design and mitigate risk.
