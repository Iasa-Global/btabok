This competency involves the selection, integration, and use of technologies, platforms, and software frameworks to deliver architectural solutions. Architects must understand how technologies interact, support business goals, and evolve over time. This includes evaluating technical capabilities, interoperability, vendor strategies, open standards, and development ecosystems. Effective architects leverage platforms and frameworks to balance innovation, risk, and long-term maintainability while aligning with enterprise architecture standards and delivery models.
