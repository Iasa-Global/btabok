Infrastructure RAS (Reliability, Availability, Serviceability) focuses on the non-functional properties of infrastructure systems that ensure consistent service delivery. Reliability refers to consistent operation over time, availability to uptime and access to services, and serviceability to ease of maintenance and repair. Architects in this competency design and assess infrastructure to meet defined quality standards, reduce unplanned outages, and streamline supportability. RAS is critical to risk mitigation, operational resilience, and long-term system viability.
