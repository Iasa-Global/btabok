Access and Identity Management (AIM) governs how users and systems are identified, authenticated, and authorized within an IT environment. It ensures that only legitimate users have access to resources in accordance with organizational policies, and that identity information is managed securely across systems. AIM is critical to security, compliance, and operational integrity. Architects in this area define integration patterns, support centralized identity services, and balance usability with risk mitigation through role-based access, federation, and multi-factor authentication.
