Information Usage focuses on how individuals and systems access, interpret, and apply information to make decisions and complete tasks. Effective information usage requires not only the availability of data but also its presentation in a way that is intuitive, contextual, and aligned with user needs. Architects must consider how information is consumed in business processes and user interactions, shaping systems to optimize discoverability, clarity, and accessibility. This competency bridges the gap between data producers and consumers, ensuring that information delivers actionable insights with minimal friction.
