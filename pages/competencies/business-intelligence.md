Business Intelligence and Information focuses on the ability to transform data into actionable insights that drive decisions and performance across the enterprise. This includes understanding how to structure, present, and govern information through dashboards, reports, analytics, and visualizations. Architects in this domain design systems that not only support analytical workloads but also ensure that information is relevant, timely, and aligned with business goals. This competency bridges technical capabilities with user needs, ensuring that data is presented in meaningful formats that support evidence-based decision-making.
