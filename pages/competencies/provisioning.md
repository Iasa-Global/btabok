Provisioning refers to the process of configuring and deploying foundational IT services—including servers, storage, network, and related infrastructure components—to meet service definitions and performance agreements. It encompasses orchestration of IT, business, and service management processes to enable timely and reliable delivery of infrastructure. With the adoption of virtualization, cloud computing, and DevOps practices, provisioning has evolved toward automation, dynamic scaling, and integration with continuous delivery pipelines. Architects must understand provisioning patterns, tools, and governance to ensure infrastructure can support agile business needs at scale.
