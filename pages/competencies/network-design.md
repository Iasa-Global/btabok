Network Design involves the architectural planning of data and voice communication infrastructure across core, distribution, and access layers. It supports the connectivity, scalability, security, and performance needs of modern enterprises. As organizations rely increasingly on integrated and real-time access to services, network design becomes foundational to IT operations and business continuity. Architects must understand internetworking concepts, topologies, protocols, and emerging technologies, ensuring that network design supports the broader infrastructure and application landscape.
