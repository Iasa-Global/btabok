Information Modeling is the architectural practice of defining the structure, semantics, and relationships of information entities within a given business domain. It enables the consistent organization, retrieval, and integration of information across platforms and processes. Information models serve as shared design artifacts that guide data lifecycle decisions and enable communication between business and technical stakeholders. Effective modeling supports flexibility, governance, and alignment with enterprise data standards, especially in environments with diverse sources such as relational databases, services, and distributed systems.
