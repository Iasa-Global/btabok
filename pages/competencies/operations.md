Operations architecture focuses on the systems, processes, and tools that ensure the IT environment runs reliably, securely, and efficiently. It includes the automation, centralization, and monitoring of operational tasks across operating systems, platforms, and infrastructure components. The goal is to maintain service continuity, manage workloads, and support business performance through robust execution of daily operations. Architects in this area align operational capabilities with architectural design, enabling observability, manageability, and resilience across the IT landscape.
